## A. Title

**Previous working-copy title:**  
“Reproducible Sparse-Concept and Calibration Diagnostics for Knowledge Tracing”

**IEEE-draft scientific title (not used as lock):**  
“Sparse-Concept Calibration of Knowledge Tracing Models for Mastery and Remediation Gates”

**Decision:** Use the preferred title. No documented editorial reason in the IJIET working files requires keeping “Mastery and Remediation Gates.” The gate experiment scores **next-response correctness** at a locked τ, not latent mastery or a classroom mastery policy. “Threshold-Based Educational Decisions” matches that operational claim without lengthening the title.

**New title:**  
Sparse-Concept Calibration of Knowledge Tracing Models for Threshold-Based Educational Decisions
