Kazimova D., Turmuratova D., Zatyneyko A., Tursyngaliyeva G., “Assessing Student Needs for AI-based Adaptive Learning in Higher Education,” *International Journal of Information and Education Technology*, vol. 16, no. 8, pp. 2068–2078, 2026. doi: 10.18178/ijiet.2026.16.8.2667  
PDF: https://www.ijiet.org/vol16/IJIET-V16N8-2667.pdf

## Front matter

- Two-column journal layout, Times-like body.
- Title; authors with numbered affiliations; emails with parenthetical initials; `*Corresponding author`.
- Running header: `International Journal of Information and Education Technology, Vol. 16, No. 8, 2026`
- doi line and page number.
- `Manuscript received …; revised …; accepted …; published …` (leave dates blank until production).
- `Abstract—` (em dash, IEEE style).
- `Keywords—` (em dash; lowercase except proper nouns).

## Body

- Roman numbered sections: I. INTRODUCTION, II. METHODOLOGY, III. RESULTS, IV. DISCUSSION, V. CONCLUSION.
- Lettered subsections: A., B., C.
- Tables captioned `Table 1. Title` (above table).
- Figures captioned `Fig. 1. Title` (below figure).
- Numbered citations `[1]`.

## Back matter (unnumbered headings)

- CONFLICT OF INTEREST
- AUTHOR CONTRIBUTIONS (initials + roles; “All authors approved…”)
- FUNDING (omit if none)
- REFERENCES (IEEE numbered)
- CC BY 4.0 copyright line
