Table S2 is the complete within-KC controlled-sparsification grid (the 12-row main-text extract was removed; the main paper keeps one pointer sentence). They are not printed in the main PDF. Locked ASSISTments T-KT ECE $0.1136$/$0.2280$ are verified, not retrained, by \texttt{scripts/rebuild\_locked\_tables.sh}.
