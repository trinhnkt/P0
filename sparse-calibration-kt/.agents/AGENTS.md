# Project guidelines

- Living manuscript: `IJIET_FINAL_REVISION/`. OJS pack: `IJIET_FINAL_REVISION/output/OJS_UPLOAD/`.
- Do not upload `_archive/` (withdrawn JEDM sources and conversion snapshots).
- T-KT is a local Transformer KT baseline, not published SimpleKT.
- Do not invent AI versions, IRB, or publisher DOI `10.18178/ijiet`.
- Do not name JEDM in the article. Cover letter records the withdrawal.
